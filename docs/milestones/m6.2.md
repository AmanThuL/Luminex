# M6.2 — Native TAA and Exposure Stability

**Status**: Implemented (2026-09-08)

M6.2 turns M6.1's motion and history inputs into a stable moving image: native-resolution TAA that
reprojects, rejects, clips and accumulates; a per-pixel reactive weight for content that must not
accumulate; exposure that adapts at a bounded rate and corrects history for the exposure it was
recorded at; and inspectable raw, reprojected and accumulated signals. `NativeTaa` is now the
default reconstruction and temporal is on by default in the App; with temporal off the frame
declares exactly what M6.1 declared, and fixed-camera output stays byte-identical.

**RHI** grows one format: `Format::R8Unorm` — colour-renderable, sampled, one byte per pixel, not a
storage or swapchain format — mapped to `MTL::PixelFormatR8Unorm`. A `[gpu][rhi]` case renders a
three-attachment MRT (`BGRA8Unorm` primary, `RG16Float` and `R8Unorm` extras) and reads all three
back; the render graph's own renderable-format predicate was brought into agreement with the RHI's.

**Render** adds the reconstruction stage, `Source/Render/TemporalResolve.h/.cpp`, composed by the
`Renderer` rather than inherited from (ADR 0014). `ReconstructionMode` selects `Raw` (the M6.1 copy,
kept as the reference bloom/display read) or `NativeTaa` (the resolve's output becomes the colour
history directly, no copy). Two `RGBA16Float` colour slots and two `D32Float` depth slots ping-pong
by declared temporal frame parity, so every temporal frame's colour slot holds that frame's output
in either mode — the invariant that makes a mode switch not a reset. `Shaders/TemporalResolve.slang`
runs 8×8 threadgroups with one invocation per pixel: dilated motion (closest depth of the current 3×3), a
dilated disocclusion test against the previous frame's expected view distance, a five-tap
Catmull-Rom history fetch corrected by the exposure ratio, YCoCg neighbourhood clipping, and an
inverse-luminance blend whose weight rises with motion and saturates to 1 on any rejection or a
fully reactive pixel. `lmx.render.reactive` (`R8Unorm`, the scene pass's third attachment) carries
that reactive weight, written from the pre-exposed emissive term. The exposure buffer grows to
`{applied, previous}`; `ExposureSeed.slang` shifts and sets on every manual temporal frame and on
auto-mode reset frames, and `ExposureResolve.slang` bounds adaptation to independent up/down rates
in stops per second. `pbrNeutralToneMap` moved into `Shaders/Tonemap.slang`, shared by
`DisplayTransform.slang` and the debug view, without changing the display pass's output. Four new
`TemporalDebugView` entries (`ReprojectedHistory`, `RejectionMask`, `BlendWeight`, `HistoryAge`,
alongside M6.1's `Off`/`MotionVectors`/`ReprojectionError`, seven in total) read the resolve's
diagnostics, declared as graph transients only when a view sinks them. New goldens: `frame-temporal-raw.txt`;
`frame-temporal.txt` regenerated for the `NativeTaa` shape; `frame-temporal-off.txt` unchanged.

**Engine** adds `SceneAnimation::EmissiveTrack`/`EmissiveKey` (held between keys, clamped outside
the range, sampled by `sampleEmissiveTrack`) and `SceneObject::emissiveStrength`, which
`Scene::animate` samples and `Scene::view()` multiplies into a copy of the draw item's authored
emissive colour without touching `Scene::materials`. TemporalLab gains a `1.5×0.75` emissive sign at
`(0, 2.5, −6)` whose strength steps `0 → 4` every 3 s, and drops its once-only editor-default
override — the scene now opens showing the accumulated picture like every other scene.

**App** flips the default: `EditorRenderSettings::temporalEnabled`/`jitterEnabled` default `true`,
`reconstruction` defaults `NativeTaa`, and the Inspector's Temporal block gains a Reconstruction
combo and the seven-item debug-view list; the Exposure block gains adapt-up/adapt-down sliders
(0–16 stops/s) while auto exposure is on. `AppOptions::temporal` becomes `TemporalMode {Off, Raw,
Taa}`; `--temporal <off|raw|taa>` defaults to `taa`, `--temporal-view` accepts the seven view names
and conflicts with `--temporal off`, and the usage line documents `--frames N` as the warmup
control.

## Deliberate limits and deviations

- **At this boundary, bloom reconstruction remained nearest-neighbor.** Its low-resolution chain and final display
  composition expose coarse rectangular halos around bright emissive content. This predates native
  TAA; bloom runs after reconstruction, so temporal accumulation does not filter the added halo.
  Filtered bloom upsampling and composition are a separate follow-up; the existing bloom output is
  preserved for this slice's temporal-off parity contract. The subsequent
  [bloom and reflection correction](../postmortems/2026-09-08-blocky-bloom-and-reflections.md)
  records the filtered replacement without rewriting these historical hashes.
- **The five-tap history fetch now normalizes its retained weights.** Dropping the four corner
  taps without normalization lost up to 1.5625% of a constant radiance under diagonal subpixel
  motion. A regression inspects the reprojected signal before clipping: the old path changed
  display RGB 181 to 179; the corrected path preserves 181. No frozen tolerance or resolve
  constant changed; the table and capture hashes above were regenerated.
- **Diagnostic writes are independently gated.** The original shared boolean wrote both outputs
  when only one transient was declared, including the untracked 1×1 fallback. Per-output mask bits
  now guard every store; poison-texture tests cover both reset and valid-history frames.
- **Emissive-only clips participate in App playback.** Both the editor and screenshot paths use
  `engine::hasAnimationTracks`, so a clip need not contain rigid or camera motion to advance.
  A regression advances an emissive-only clip from its off key to its on key.
- **The motion pipelines' reactive attachment format was left undeclared for part of this slice, a
  defect found and fixed before it shipped.** The motion pipelines carried only the motion extra
  format (`extraColorCount = 1`), so the reactive attachment's
  pipeline format stayed `Invalid`; the reactive channel read 0 everywhere, Metal validation never
  diagnosed this pipeline/pass attachment mismatch in the observed validation run, and
  the emissive scenario passed anyway because a uniform quad's neighbourhood clip collapses onto
  this frame's colour regardless of the reactive weight. Fixed to `{kMotionFormat, kReactiveFormat}`,
  count 2; the emissive scenario was then strengthened to assert the `Reactive` reason and blend
  weight 1 at the quad centre, demonstrated RED against the old declaration first. **Follow-up**:
  the RHI has no check
  that a bound pipeline's extra-attachment count and formats match the open render pass's —
  `Metal4CommandList` retains no pass attachments and `Metal4Pipeline` no `extraColorCount`; closing
  this gap needs retained state on both plus a `Validate` entry point.
- **The disocclusion test dilates both sides**, not the point sample spec §6 step 3 originally
  specified — see the spec's 2026-09-08 amendment and ADR 0014. `kDisocclusionTolerance` stays 0.05.
- **A rate of 0 is a `rate > 0` guard**, not the spec's literal `clamp(…, −0, +0)`, which would
  freeze exposure instead of disabling adaptation; see the spec's amendment.
- **The auto → manual correction ratio is exact only up to one adaptation step** (≤ rate·dt — 0.05
  stops at the default up rate, zero once auto has settled), because the auto resolve leaves index 0
  holding the value the *next* frame applies, not the transition frame's own value. Accepted as a
  limit; see the spec's amendment.
- **The exposure-reset non-unification stands.** `TemporalSettings::sceneGeneration` bumps on any
  content change, so deriving the exposure reset from it would restart metering on every Inspector
  drag — a behaviour change nothing in this slice asked for. The blend's exposure correction already
  absorbs the discontinuity a real exposure reset produces. `App/ExposureReset.h` is untouched.
- **`TemporalInputs` gains `colorSlot`, and `recordFrame` gains `historyValid`**, beyond spec §3's
  original struct shapes — both documented in the spec's amendment and needed for the stage to name
  its own output handle and to avoid mis-declaring a WAW edge as a WAR one under `Raw`.
- **Both depth slots import `ShaderRead`, not `RenderTarget`.** The resolve samples both depth slots
  every `NativeTaa` frame, so `ShaderRead` is their true terminal use; this is the same conservative
  rule M6.1 already applied to its single depth target, not a new one.
- **Per-pixel history age saturates at 2048** in the colour history's half-precision alpha channel
  in practice, though `kMaxHistoryAge`/the CPU `historyAge` are defined as 65535: a `float16`'s
  precision loses integer resolution above roughly 2048, so the diagnostic alpha channel and the
  CPU-counted `TemporalStatus::historyAge` (which drives `warmupComplete` and the tests) diverge at
  very long unbroken histories. The CPU count is authoritative; this is a per-pixel diagnostic
  artefact only.
- **Colour-slot imports follow the final consumer (ADR 0015).** NativeTaa records `ShaderRead`
  after bloom/display, and Raw + HistoryAge records `ShaderRead` after the debug view; other Raw
  frames record `CopyDestination`. ADR 0015 supersedes only ADR 0014's writer-use examples,
  preserving its ownership and recorded-use invariant.
- **`kReactiveEmissiveScale` and the rejection reason codes are each defined in more than one
  place** — the constant in `Source/Render/TemporalResolve.h`, `Shaders/ScenePass.slang` and
  `Shaders/ScenePassAuto.slang` (the two shaders that write the reactive attachment), the reason
  codes in `TemporalResolve.slang` and mirrored by the header for the debug view's legend and the
  tests' readback — because a shader and its host-side mirror cannot share one definition in this
  toolchain; the two shaders' copies of `kReactiveEmissiveScale` each carry a comment naming the
  header as the source of truth, and the header's own comment describes what the constant does
  rather than naming the shaders back.
- **`TemporalResolve.cpp` is about 600 lines**, holding four pass declarations plus the slot
  allocation and pipelines undivided. Flagged rather than split, since no natural seam separates the
  declarations without duplicating the slot/pipeline state they close over.
- **`TemporalEditorState::onSceneSelected` keeps two now-unused parameters** (`settings`, `id`)
  after the TemporalLab once-only default branch was removed, so its signature and call sites stay
  unchanged; the parameters are named in the header for documentation and left unnamed at the
  definition.
- **`RejectionMask`'s `Clipped` flag (added green) is not visibly distinguishable from plain
  `Reactive` yellow** by eye — a 0.5 green addition on a yellow flat colour reads close to yellow
  itself. The encoding is correct and machine-readable; a manual QA check would likely flag the
  visual ambiguity, which is one reason that check is left for the owner.
- **`Tools/GpuDebug/gputrace_dump.py`'s pre-existing stale checks continue unchanged**: the
  absent-resource-name and shadow-transform-recompute errors M6.1 recorded still fire, and the
  format-decoder gap now also covers `R8Unorm` blobs (the reactive target) alongside the M6.1-era
  `RG16Float`/`RGBA16Float` gap. Tooling staleness, not a rendering regression.
