### Bounded input diagnosis

an external same-harness parent/candidate probe over 32 frames, with sampled depth/reactive,
HDR/motion/display readback and symmetric per-frame waits. It is supplementary diagnosis, not the
strict acceptance run. The report describes its earlier five-failure snapshot; the reviewed matrix
above owns that reviewed result.

- Parent-to-parent Sponza MetalFX 0.5 and Helmet MetalFX 1 controls were exact throughout all
  32 per-frame hashes and retained first/final targets. These bounded controls do not support
  attributing candidate differences to ordinary run variance.
- Sponza's diagnostic pair ended with identical HDR/depth/motion/reactive but different display;
  sparse HDR differences occurred earlier. Native TAA spread those differences much less. This is
  consistent with reconstruction history preserving/spreading sparse inputs, not proof of a
  particular compiler transformation or a resolved root cause.
- Helmet ended with sparse HDR differences and numerically equal depth/motion/reactive. MaterialLab
  additionally changed first-frame motion by up to 0.314697265625 UV because of correct instance
  seeding; frame-32 motion is numerically equal. Signed-zero differences are recorded separately.
- Final depth/reactive targets are identical and no nonfinite numeric mismatch occurs. The probes
  do not partition initial-motion and later HDR contributions to recurrent MetalFX differences.
