# M6.1 — Temporal State and Motion

**Status**: Implemented (2026-09-07)

M6.1 gives the frame trustworthy motion and one persistent, reset-aware history, opt-in behind
`SceneView::temporal.enabled`. With it off, the frame declares exactly what M5.5 declared, and
fixed-camera output stays byte-identical.

**RHI** grows additively: `RenderPassDesc` gains `ExtraColorTarget extraColor[kMaxExtraColorTargets]`
(`kMaxExtraColorTargets = 3`) and `extraColorCount`; `GraphicsPipelineDesc` gains matching
`extraColorFormats`/count. `RG16Float` is now colour-renderable and readable
(`Validate::bytesPerPixel` returns 4), which is what lets tests read motion back. The Metal 4 backend
maps extra index *i* to `colorAttachments[i+1]`, sharing the existing residency and barrier
mechanisms with no new bookkeeping. Every `[checkpoint-a]` source is unchanged.

**Render** defines the temporal contract once. `Source/Render/Temporal.h` holds `FrameExtents`, a
1-based Halton(2,3) jitter sequence (`haltonJitterPixels`, values strictly inside `(-0.5, 0.5)` on
each axis), `CameraFrameState`/`buildCameraFrameState`, `MotionClass`, and `kMotionFormat =
RG16Float`; the motion convention (`motion = uvCurrent - uvPrevious` from unjittered clip positions,
`uv = ndc.xy * (0.5, -0.5) + 0.5` over the render extent, sentinel `+inf`) is shared with
`Shaders/Motion.slang`. `Source/Render/TemporalHistory.h` derives `HistoryResetReason` in the fixed
order `None`/`FirstFrame`/`TemporalEnabled`/`SceneChanged`/`ExtentChanged`/`ProjectionChanged`/
`CameraCut` from a `FrameSignature`. `RenderGraph::PassDesc` gains `extraColor`; `GraphDump` prints
an extra as `color attachment[1]`. The `Renderer` declares, on the temporal path: `lmx.pass.scene`
with motion as attachment 1 (sky writes rotation-only motion from the previous eye);
`lmx.pass.temporal.reproject` (compute, culled without a debug-view sink) reading history, scene
colour and motion into a transient diagnostic; `lmx.pass.temporal.debugView` (a **raster** pass after
display, not compute — the display target's `BGRA8Unorm` format carries no storage-write usage);
`lmx.pass.temporal.commitHistory` (copy) overwriting history from scene colour.
`lmx.render.historyColor` imports with `previousUse = CopyDestination`; the motion and scene-colour imports state
the recorded previous temporal frame's actual terminal use. Uniform blocks grew append-only:
`PassUniforms` 272→400, `ObjectUniforms` 304→368, `SkyUniforms` 96→176 (the sky reuses its tail
padding for the jitter NDC offset rather than carrying a second matrix). A dedicated clamp sampler,
`lmx.render.temporalSampler`, serves history reads. New goldens: `frame-mrt.txt`,
`frame-temporal.txt`, `frame-temporal-off.txt`.

**Engine** adds previous transforms and rigid animation. `SceneObject` gains `previousModel`/
`motionClass`; `Scene::resetMotion()`, `commitFrame()`, `advanceAnimation(dt)`, and
`animate(seconds)` (sampling `SceneAnimation`'s `RigidTrack`/`CameraKey` tracks at a fixed 1/60 s
step) drive them. The glTF loader bakes `animations[0]`'s LINEAR and STEP TRS channels to
world-space 60 Hz keys (`kAnimationBakeRate`); STEP clips are resampled as linear keys; CUBICSPLINE,
skins, morph targets and non-decomposable transforms fail the load as `Unsupported`.
`decomposeTransform` is now the single robust factorisation used by both the loader and runtime
animation, rejecting shear and accepting one zero-scale axis. `SceneEnvironment.h` extracts the
shared sky/light rig that three scenes had duplicated. The catalog grows to five scenes: `milk-truck`
(Sample, CesiumMilkTruck, CC BY 4.0) and `temporal-lab` (Diagnostic — a checkerboard floor, a
rotating cube, an orbiting sphere, oscillating poles and one `Invalid`-flagged cube, numbers in
`TemporalLab.cpp`'s header). `Assets/Fetched/InterpolationTest` (CC0) is a sampler-conformance test
fixture. `engine::loadGltfScene(device, path, name)` is now a public entry point.

**App** exposes all of it: `EditorRenderSettings` gains the temporal toggles; the pure
`TemporalEditorState` owns the scene-generation counter, camera-cut latch and TemporalLab's
once-only defaults; the Inspector's Temporal block has toggles, a debug-view combo,
Play/Pause/Step/Reset time (with `resetMotion()` and a camera cut), Follow camera track, a Camera
cut button and read-only status; the frame loop advances animation at a fixed 1/60 s per declared
frame and calls `Scene::commitFrame()` after `execute()` (a skipped frame does neither); `--frames
N`, `--temporal`, and `--temporal-view off|motion|reprojection` drive offscreen captures.

**Manual visual QA**, from the four-frame capture bitmaps only; the interactive checks below are not

TemporalLab's slow per-frame motion as faint tints rather than vivid colour — the `Invalid` cube
reads clearly magenta, and on the truck the wheels are visibly tinted while the body reads flat grey
(consistent with a stationary rigid part under a moving camera-relative view); the Reprojection view
shows silhouette edges and checker moiré where expected. Left for the owner: the truck's wheels
showing motion while the camera itself is still, a camera cut visibly blanking the Reprojection view
for one frame, and dragging an object in the Inspector showing motion for that object only — none of
these are exercisable from static four-frame captures.

## Deliberate limits and deviations

- **The debug view is a raster pass, not the compute pass spec §6 named.** `BGRA8Unorm` (the display
  target's format, required by the swapchain and `--screenshot`) carries no storage-write usage
  under this RHI's `isStorageFormat`, so a compute pass cannot write it. A fullscreen triangle
  overwrites every texel identically and, since the display target is not an sRGB view, lands the
  spec's encodings unchanged. The spec is amended accordingly.
- **The history import states `previousUse = CopyDestination`, not `ShaderRead`.** The previous
  temporal frame's actual last access to `lmx.render.historyColor` is the commit copy, so declaring
  a prior read would leave the cross-frame read-after-write unbarriered — a prior read only orders
  ahead of this frame's first write, not its first read. `CopyDestination` is what makes the graph
  emit the transition ahead of the reprojection pass. Recorded in ADR 0013 and the amended spec.
- **`SkyUniforms` reuses its tail padding for the jitter NDC offset** rather than carrying a second
  matrix, keeping the specified 176-byte size; the sky's motion vertex entry point applies jitter to
  the rasterised position instead.
- **The Halton jitter sequence is 1-based** (radical inverses at `index % 16 + 1`), not the spec's
  literal `[-0.5, 0.5)`, so the worst jitter in the set is the corner sample rather than the origin;
  values fall strictly inside `(-0.5, 0.5)` on each axis. The spec's interval wording is amended.
- **`GraphDump` prints an extra colour attachment as `color attachment[1]`**, not the design note's
  `color[1]`, for consistency with the existing `color attachment` vocabulary the primary already
  uses; goldens are byte-identical to this spelling.
- **glTF STEP animation clips are baked as linear 60 Hz keys**, not held-step keys, so a STEP clip's
  intended discontinuity is approximated by fine-grained linear interpolation between very close
  samples rather than reproduced exactly.
- **Sponza screenshot output showed run-to-run byte noise once, on the unmodified M5.5 baseline,
  before any M6.1 code existed** (1 of 3 reruns differed by 86 bytes, `|delta| = 1`); it was outside
  M6.1's scope then and was not reproduced on the final tip, where all three captured runs matched
  the baseline hash exactly.
- **`decomposeTransform` uses an absolute 1e-4 degeneracy threshold**, which can silently drop
  rotation information for a uniformly tiny (near-zero) scale rather than reporting it as invalid.
- **`GltfLoader.cpp` now includes `Scene.h`**, a small layering inversion (a lower-level Engine file
  depending on a higher-level one); a small `Engine/Transform.h` would restore the boundary but was
  not required for this slice.
- **History and motion-target allocations are retained when temporal is disabled**, not freed, so a
  scene that toggles temporal on and back off keeps paying its GPU footprint; `TemporalStatus::
  historyBytes` reports the retained cost.
- **Jitter is unconsumed by any shipped reconstruction pass in M6.1.** `temporal.jitterEnabled` only
  perturbs the rasterised scene and sky positions to prove the motion convention excludes it; no
  pass in this slice reads a jittered target as an accumulation input. That is M6.2's job.
- **Render-graph transient sizes are baked into the two `[gpu]` goldens** (`frame-mrt.txt`,
  `frame-temporal.txt`); a future device reporting different heap sizes for the same declarations
  would need those goldens regenerated, not just re-verified.
- **`--frames`, `--temporal` and `--temporal-view` are accepted and silently ignored in windowed
  mode**, consistent with the existing `--windowed`/`--screenshot` flag relationship — they only
  affect the offscreen `--screenshot` path.
- **The GPU capture dump tool (`Tools/GpuDebug/gputrace_dump.py`) reports pre-existing anomalies
  unrelated to M6.1.** Its "`lmx.render.sceneColor` is absent" and shadow-transform-mismatch errors
  reproduce identically on a control capture of the unchanged MaterialLab scene: the tool still
  expects a resource name from before the display-transform pass was introduced, and its shadow
  recompute predates a later shadow fix. Both are tooling staleness, not M6.1 regressions. Its
  warnings are a decoder gap for `RG16Float` (format 65) and `RGBA16Float` (format 115) blobs, which
  now includes the motion and history targets alongside the pre-existing HDR scene colour and IBL
  textures the tool already could not decode; this is a tooling follow-up, not evidence that those
  targets hold incorrect data.
- **The Motion debug view's fixed gain (`R,G = 0.5 + motion * 8`) under-represents TemporalLab's
  slow per-frame motion** as faint tints rather than strongly saturated colour; a configurable gain
  is a follow-up, not part of this slice.
- **A pre-existing double-shutdown in `EditorShell::create`'s initial-scene-failure path**, recorded
  since M5.3, still stands and remains outside this slice's scope.
