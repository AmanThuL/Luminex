## Delivered behavior

- The top toolbar selects Scene or Measure and provides one Play/Pause button that switches icons with state, plus separate Stop and Step icons.
  Camera-rail follow lives in its options; unavailable rails retain a disabled reason.
- Scenes load Stopped. First Scene Play captures camera/time and animation-owned object
  poses/emissive strength. Pause stops advancement; Step advances 1/60 s and pauses.
  Stop restores the captured state and resets motion, temporal and exposure history.
  Scene switches restore the old run before activation. Static scenes support camera preview.
- Measure Play starts the existing deterministic warmup/sample plan. Pause is disabled for this
  uninterrupted run. Stop cancels; cancellation and completion restore preview state while
  keeping the report. Performance retains plan counts, results and Export.
- The preview shares the existing scene. Rendering settings and unrelated scene edits are
  outside restoration; no independent runtime world or full scene-edit rollback was added.
  CLI behavior is unchanged. The [guide](../guides/gpu-debugging.md#editor-playback) owns operation.
