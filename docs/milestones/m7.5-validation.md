## Retained limits

Local lights are unshadowed and affect opaque/masked surfaces only. Area lights, transparency and
glTF punctual import remain absent. Intensity is relative, not absolute photometry. Fixed grid,
slice distances/capacities and deterministic overflow retain their stated limits. Tiny/far light
floating-point behavior beyond the tested domain remains a review risk; the independent property
fixture and finite captured rails are evidence for their measured scope, not a universal proof.
