# M5.1 — RHI Execution-Model Decision

**Status**: Implemented (2026-08-12)

M5.1 ran a pre-registered experiment deciding the RHI's execution-model direction. A
GPU-address-first prototype (`Experiments/NoApi/`, namespace `lmx::experimental::noapi`, non-default targets)
was built on Metal 4 and compared against the maintained RHI on one frozen thirteen-pass
representative graph — carrying a scene-pass slice that replicates the full production binding
surface over synthetic assets — plus frozen binding-scale, hazard, lifetime, indirect, and misuse
workloads. Baseline, hypotheses, workload scales, measurement protocol, rubric, adoption
thresholds, and the prototype's disposal were all frozen in the design spec before measurement.
ADR 0010 records the outcome: **partial reshape** — production adopts an address-first per-frame
data path as a bounded change to the binding/uniform-delivery contract area via a separately
accepted M5.x migration before M6; the object-shaped resource, pass, barrier, residency, and
pipeline model is retained, and the render graph keeps logical ownership.

## Evidence

- Checkpoint A (ADR 0009, 18 cases, 1,749 assertions) passed unchanged under `MTL_DEBUG_LAYER=1`
  at the milestone start, after the prototype landed, and at the tip; production runtime sources
  and public contracts were never modified — the build description gained only the non-default
  experiment targets.
- Both encoders execute the same frozen graph: 30 of 32 frames byte-identical, with a recorded
  3-byte single-ULP scene-pass code-generation deviation whose mechanism was isolated (identical
  arithmetic, different operand provenance) rather than resolved by weakening the frozen gate.
  Each encoder is independently bit-deterministic across runs, validation-clean.
- Scored under the frozen paired protocol (12 one-process AB/BA repetitions, 16+256 frames,
  release, validation off, AC power, environment recorded): CPU encoding −96.2% on the graph;
  binding-scale CPU regressions of 8.5% and 10.8% are below the 25% material threshold. Full-model
  graph binding calls are −60.0% but are not relied on for the bounded reshape; the incumbent's
  1,025 per-frame buffer creations compare with the
  prototype's 9 for a full run. API surface (+33–108% across the frozen counting rules) and
  isolated-hazard barrier counts (+50–150%) are material regressions for the prototype. Rounds 1
  and 2 were invalidated and withdrawn before this third collection. Full tables:
  `docs/research/2026-08-12-execution-model-evidence.md`.
- S-BIND's conservative prototype encoding also produces 2× binding calls and nonzero per-frame
  root bytes against prebuilt incumbent data. The bounded reshape does not adopt that static-data
  behavior or the prototype's bindless resource path; the production API must combine ring
  allocation, copying, and address binding while preserving static reuse.
- The hazard matrix passed 24/24 on the prototype and 19/24 on the incumbent — five per-mip
  raster cases are inexpressible through `rhi::RenderPassDesc` (no attachment subresource
  selection), recorded as an incumbent capability gap. All six misuse contracts assert cleanly on
  the prototype; the incumbent's destroyed-handle case is undefined behavior rather than a
  diagnosable assert. Both are recorded incumbent findings, not experiment failures.
- Metal 4 and D3D12 mappings for every prototype concept are classified
  native/emulated/unavailable/unknown with shader constraints (Slang expresses the address-first
  binding model via the struct-member bindless form; the raw texture-pointer form is rejected by
  the Metal compiler), grounded in the built prototype on the Metal side and a documented paper
  mapping on the D3D12 side; Vulkan is an unscored comparative column.

## Deliberate limits

**The experiment is a frozen artifact, not a product.** `Experiments/NoApi/` stays non-default
and `Frozen — non-normative` per the disposal policy frozen before measurement; the adopted
change is reimplemented cleanly in production by the M5.x migration, never promoted from the
experiment.

**The parity gate was accepted under the roadmap's claims-scoped wording.** ADR 0010's owner
disposition (2026-08-12) accepts gate 1 as recorded — 30 of 32 frames byte-identical plus three
bytes of isolated single-ULP code-generation rounding, never described as strict byte identity —
and preserves the strict-reading counterfactual under which the decision would have fallen back
to retain.

**Scored numbers are one machine, one scale.** All measurements are from a single M3 Max under a
wait-idle-per-frame protocol that isolates CPU encoding; the API-surface counts cover the
interface subsets the frozen workloads exercise, not hypothetical larger renderers. The evidence
document records the environment and every caveat.

**Resident memory is inconclusive.** The frozen metric requires Metal-reported sizes on both sides,
but the incumbent public RHI exposes no such query and production contracts could not change during
the experiment. A requested-byte proxy (+76% for the prototype) is retained as explicitly unscored
descriptive evidence; allocation creation counts remain exact and scored. Resident memory is
non-core and did not affect the partial-reshape threshold.

**The incumbent's encode cost is dominated by one structural fact.** Per-draw uniforms beyond the
backend's fixed ring capacity cost per-frame buffer creation through the public API (evidence
7.1). The M5.x migration exists precisely to close that; re-measuring after migration is expected
to shrink the headline graph delta substantially.
