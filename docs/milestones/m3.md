# M3 — Scene Content and Forward Lighting

**Status**: Implemented (2026-08-08)

M3 added the Lumine parity scene, Khronos Sponza and DamagedHelmet samples, DDS/glTF/text-model
loading, procedural geometry, normal mapping, cubemap reflection, directional lighting, shadow
mapping with PCF/PCSS, sky rendering, and live scene/light/render controls.

Lighting math is linear. Authored colors decode at scene construction; shader output encodes once.
`SceneView` is the Engine-produced, Render-consumed frame boundary. Three frames remain in flight
with checked transient-resource recycle behavior.

Evidence at close: loader and math tests, GPU smoke coverage, Metal validation, fixed-scene images,
and the frame walkthrough. Known baseline limits include positional scene selection, synchronous
large-scene loading, a private ImGui backend integration, generic pass labels, and missing active
glTF scene hierarchy traversal. M3.1 owns those baseline repairs.
