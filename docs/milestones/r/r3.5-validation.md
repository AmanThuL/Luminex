## Capture decoder limits

The frozen parent dump contains an existing `missing-expected-resource` error because its anomaly
rule still expects `lmx.render.sceneColor` while the schema names `lmx.render.sceneColorHdr`.
Unsupported formats remain undecodable, and uniform blob attribution reports six candidates and
stays unresolved. The candidate must preserve these findings; equal layouts are established by
the schema and equal encoder order by decoded CS/setLabel records joined to the graph schedule.
The capture gate does not claim complete texture or uniform-value decoding. Capture paths, blob
object IDs, timings and pixel statistics are excluded from semantic comparison; raw schemas and
graphs remain byte gates in part A. Part B alone may canonicalize object/layout registry order as
the design declares; no label, descriptor, uniform layout or upload order is dropped.
