# R3.2 — Shader Folders Validation Record

**Status**: Implemented

`Shaders/` has two levels. `Common/` holds the eight shared modules `Modules/` held,
`Passes/<family>/` holds the ten pass families with their family-local modules, and `Tests/`
keeps the thirteen oracles unchanged. 63 Slang files, none at the root. `xmake/shaders.lua` and
`Tools/check_shader_imports.py` know the layout; the runtime still loads `Shaders/<basename>`
and no `loadShaderLibrary` call changed.

Against the [exit gate](../../roadmap/codebase-restructuring.md#r32--shader-folders): generated
MSL is identical to the parent's at every gated commit once `#line` directives are dropped, the
`.metal`/`.metallib` name inventories are unchanged, the runtime-MSL fallback still resolves
every shader with no metallib present, basename collisions and cross-family imports are still
rejected, and `xmake format --check` held at every gated commit.

Parent for every comparison: `e9c6e30`, the tip of `main`. Candidate: `ac31f90`, the branch head.
Device: Apple M3 Max, macOS 26.5.2, Metal 4, release builds, xmake 3.0.9.

## Evidence bundle

and inventory trees beside it, `parent/` the frozen baseline, `head/` the head protocol, plus

it is a scratch location outside the tree and is not archived.

## The per-commit gate

`gate.sh` logs `git rev-parse HEAD` and `git status --porcelain`, refuses a dirty tree and refuses
a label that already holds evidence, then per commit:

1. builds `App`, `Tests` and `FrameDataBench` in release;
2. writes each target's 63 `.metal` files with `#line` lines stripped and diffs the three
   directories against the frozen parent's; diffs each target's 126-line `.metal`/`.metallib`
   name inventory and the 849-line Catch2 case list against the parent's;
3. runs `check_shader_imports.py`, `check_project_policy.py`, `check_module_deps.py`,
   `check_source_headers.py` and `check_cpp_layout.py`;
4. runs the `Tools/tests` Python suite, `xmake format --check`, and the Catch2 CPU filter `~[gpu]`.

Every passing log ends `All tests passed (903868 assertions in 650 test cases)` then
`GATE PASS <label>`.

That is five of the seven checkers `xmake policy` runs. `check_cpp_comments.py` needs a
per-worktree `compile_commands.json` and `check_submodule_pin.py` needs the network; both were
deferred to the head, where the seven-checker comparison below covers them. R3.2 changes no
submodule pin.

The gate is proven to fail: with an sRGB threshold constant altered in `Shaders/Modules/`'s
`Encode.slang`, the normalized-MSL diff reports `DisplayTransform.metal.norm` and
`TemporalDebugView.metal.norm` (`gate/self-fail.log`, `gate/self-fail-v2.log`). Those are the
only two generated files carrying that constant — not `Encode.metal.norm`, which the plan
predicted, since `Encode` is a module and generates no library of its own. A dirty tree is
refused before any build (`gate/dirty-refusal-check.log`).

The importer probe shows that both moved modules are still tracked in the build's dependency
graph and that no stale artifact survives a move. It does **not** demonstrate that the rule
sweeps family folders for oracle sources: `Passes/Visibility/Occlusion.slang` is itself a target
source, so touching it rebuilds all 63 either way. What proves the sweep is that
`Shaders/Tests/OcclusionProbe.slang` compiles at all: its `import Occlusion;` resolves only
through `-I Shaders/Passes/Visibility`, and it is the only one of the five distinct oracle
imports that reaches outside `Common/`.

The probe also measures rebuild granularity at target level, not per source file: any tracked
`.slang` change rebuilds every shader of that target. That is the rule's conservative design, not
a regression.

## Declared metallib and `.metal` byte difference

Of the 126 files in each build's `Shaders/` output directory, 104 differ byte-wise between parent
and candidate: all 63 `.metallib`, because `-frecord-sources` embeds the new source paths, plus 41
of the 63 `.metal`, whose `#line` directives carry those paths. The remaining 22 `.metal` files
are byte-identical. Stripping `#line` lines from the 41 leaves zero differing lines in every one
of them. Despite 104 differing shader files, all 240 parity captures are bit-identical and the
runtime-compiled fallback render matches the metallib render byte for byte.

## Comment retargeting

One mechanical commit applied a frozen 53-row substitution map over the C++ and Slang comments
naming a shader path: 74 changed lines across 34 files, every one inside a `//` comment. 41 of
those lines then exceeded 100 columns — 23 in C++, which `xmake format` reflowed, and 18 in
Slang, which no formatter touches. Those 18 were rewrapped by hand in the same commit, wording
unchanged, only the break moved:

`Passes/Temporal/TemporalCommon.slang:3`; `Passes/Scene/ScenePass.slang:4,19`;
`Passes/Scene/ScenePassAuto.slang:16,32`; `Passes/Scene/ScenePassAutoMask.slang:17,33`;
`Passes/Scene/ScenePassMask.slang:5,20`; `Passes/Temporal/SpatialUpscale.slang:48`;
`Passes/Temporal/TemporalDebugView.slang:34`; `Passes/Temporal/TemporalReproject.slang:68`;
`Passes/Temporal/TemporalResolve.slang:17`; `Passes/Temporal/TemporalUpscale.slang:2,26,92,125,136`
(paths as they stand after the move).

One manual content fix, distinct from the rewraps: `Source/Render/SceneStage.cpp:806` named two

substituted lines because of the reflows and rewraps.

## Failed and repeated runs

- **Verification round 1 failed at the first move commit.** `check_project_policy.py` reported
  `docs/research/2026-09-06-graphics-paradigm-project-fit.md:56: broken local link:
  ../../Shaders/Modules/Lighting.slang` — one markdown link in the repository pointed at a moved
  shader, and a link and its target must move together. All four concurrent verification runs of
  that round completed their builds and failed on that single error, identically
  (`gate/move-9ccbbe1.log`, `gate/move-f0ca462.log`, `gate/move-924900b.log`,
  `gate/move-9c39e03.log`). The error is the complete policy failure set at that commit, not the
  first of several.
- **The chain was rewritten to fix it, and every hash changed.** A `docs:` commit placed in front
  of the move chain de-links the reference while keeping its path text verbatim as inline code,
  so the move commits stay renames-only and no frozen research note loses a written path. The
  abandoned chain remains reachable from the ref `r3.2-pre-relink`. Round 2 gated all twelve
  hashes: 12/12 `GATE PASS`.
- **The plan's worktree recipe was incomplete.** It omits
  `xmake project -k compile_commands -P .`, which three of the gate's five checkers require; they
  exit 2 without it, and a fresh worktree never has the file. Three of the verification worktrees
  would have failed after full builds. The file is gitignored and does not dirty the gated tree.
  The recipe needs that step added.
- **Capture-sequence stdout was compared twice.** The first timestamp filter did not match this
  build's `[HH:MM:SS.mmm]` form or its leading NSLog line, so every line still differed. The
  filter was corrected and the *same saved logs* re-compared; the App was not re-run and no
  artifact was regenerated. Both outputs are retained.
- **The runtime-MSL fallback's first attempt failed on both sides.** A copied build directory sits
  outside any provisioned tree, so `findRepositoryAsset` could not reach Sponza. Fixed with an
  `Assets` symlink inside each *copy*, pointing at that side's own worktree — symmetric, and the
  same arrangement the earlier steps already used. Neither worktree was modified; the failed logs
  are kept.
- **The parity matrix's alternation was not strictly cadenced.** Order was parent, cand, cand,
  parent, cand, parent, cand, parent, then four parent-then-cand rounds; two candidate runs are
  adjacent. The parent is sampled both before and after that pair, and no case varies anywhere.

## Limits

- **Checkpoint A was executed at only 3 of its 19 cases.** `669 assertions in 3 test cases` is the
  graph half; the 16 RHI cases live in `RojoRHITests` and were run at neither parent nor
  candidate, nor was `RojoRHITests/gpu`. Mitigation, verified: `git diff --name-only e9c6e30
  ac31f90 -- RojoRHI` is empty. The `19 cases, 16 RHI` line is the inventory checker's output, not
  an execution count.
- **The fallback probe rendered BMPs, not the PNGs the spec names.** The tagged-PNG writer was
  exercised with metallibs present, not in the metallib-free configuration.
- **The parity tool's own verdict is uninformative here.** All 16 runs report `allMatched: false`,
  `0/15` against the frozen `reference.json`, exit 1 — on both builds, with identical hashes. The
  reference was captured on a different device and toolchain, and these scratch worktrees log
  `no baked mip chain` for DamagedHelmet during the captures. All evidential weight rests on the
  parent-to-candidate hash cross-comparison assembled outside the tool, not on its verdict.
- **Parity coverage is 15 cases: 3 scenes × {off, taa, vendor} × {1.0, 0.5} render scale.** It
  does not touch GPU visibility classification, HZB or occlusion, batched submission, the lighting
  diagnostic views or the selection outline. `HzbDebugView` and `FrameDataQuad` are the only two
  moved entry points not executed anywhere in the head protocol; their generated MSL is
  byte-identical to the parent's per the gate, so the residual risk is confined to
  load-by-basename, the mechanism all 63 share.
- **Determinism, not statistics, carries the parity result.** Eight runs per side is a fixed
  sample with essentially no power against nondeterminism rarer than about 1 in 8 per case. What
  it establishes is that both builds are perfectly deterministic across 8 repetitions on all 15
  cases and that the two determinism classes coincide.
- **The cross-family-import rejection used a hand-assembled `slangc` command line**, not
  `xmake build`. The flag set was read off `xmake/shaders.lua` and matches, so the inference is
  sound but is an inference; the build rule itself was exercised only for the basename collision.
- **The importer probe measures target-granularity rebuilds.** No per-family dependency edge was
  or can be measured by it.
- **The eight-round parity cap was fixed before measurement, and the claim rests on file mtimes**
  (the written cap at 21:46, the first run at 23:35) plus the absence of any gap in the 16 run
  directories' mtimes large enough to hide a deleted run. The written cap is untracked, so this is
  not a cryptographic commitment.
- **All evidence comes from one machine** and two scratch worktrees whose asset trees lack the
  DamagedHelmet baked mip chain and the MaterialLab HDRI — symmetrically on both sides.
- The precise-float selection still keys on basename prefixes, not folders, as
  [the record](r3.2.md#limits) states; `RojoRHI/Shaders/` does not move; R4 owns deduplication
  inside `Passes/Scene/`.
