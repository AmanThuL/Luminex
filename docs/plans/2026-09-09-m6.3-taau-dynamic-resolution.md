# M6.3 — TAAU and Dynamic Resolution

**Status**: In progress (2026-09-09)

> For agentic workers: execute with superpowers:subagent-driven-development, one task per fresh
> worker, review between tasks. Steps use numbered lists; the SDD ledger tracks completion.

This plan executes the accepted design in `docs/specs/2026-09-09-m6.3-taau-dynamic-resolution-design.md`.
`docs/roadmap.md` owns the M6.3 outcome, deliverables, exit gate and deferrals; this plan
decomposes that boundary without expanding it. The spec fixes the extents and jitter contract, the
reset policy, the RHI and graph growth, the allocation table, the kernels and their constants, the
controller's rule, the App surface and the frozen tolerances; tasks reference those definitions and
may not change them.

## Outcome

The renderer rasterises into an active render extent inside targets allocated at the output
extent, reconstructs the output image with a temporal upscaler (`NativeTaa`) or a spatial upscale
of the raw frame (`Raw`) at the same output extent, reuses its history across render-scale changes
and resets it only on a resize, and steps the scale against a GPU budget through a pure controller
the App drives. At scale 1 the frame is M6.2's, declaration for declaration; with temporal off it
is M5.5's.

## Global constraints

- C++23; `Result<T>` for creation, `LMX_ASSERT` for misuse; every GPU object labelled.
- RHI growth is additive: no existing public field or signature changes, and every
  `[checkpoint-a]` test source is untouched.
- With `SceneView::temporal.enabled == false` the frame declares exactly the M6.2 passes, imports
  and attachments; at `renderScale == 1` it declares exactly the M6.2 temporal frame, and
  `Shaders/TemporalResolve.slang` keeps its compiled output.
- Uniform blocks grow append-only; every `static_assert` size and
  `registerUniformLayoutsForCapture` entry is updated with them.
- Constants, pass names, shader entry points and tolerances are the spec's (sections 3, 7, 8, 10);
  shaders and tests cite `Temporal.h`/`TemporalResolve.h` and never restate numbers.
- Source comments avoid process words the policy checker rejects (task numbers, model names).
- Commit subjects use the convention scopes, ≤ 72 characters; no AI trailer.

```cpp
namespace lmx::rhi {
struct RenderPassDesc { /* existing fields unchanged */
    /// Origin-anchored viewport and scissor; 0/0 means the whole attachment. Both zero or both
    /// non-zero, neither larger than the attachments' extent. The load action still clears the
    /// whole attachment, so texels outside the area hold the clear value.
```

`TemporalStatus` fields only), `Source/Render/TemporalResolve.h` (`TemporalInputs` field and the

`kUpscaleMinWeight = 0.25f`. `buildCameraFrameState` asserts `render <= output` on both axes,

−0.125})` is `{0.25, 0.125}`, and projecting a world point through `viewProjectionJittered` and
`viewProjection` of one `CameraFrameState` gives texel positions differing by exactly that offset
within 1e-4; `renderSamplePosition({3, 2}, 1280×720 at scale 0.5, jitter j)` is `(1.75, 1.25) +
jitterTexelOffset(j)` and at scale 1 is `(3.5, 2.5) + offset`; the projection matrix at scale 0.5
is bit-identical to the one at scale 1 while the jittered matrices differ by the render-extent
NDC scale; the existing "any extent field change resets" case becomes "an output extent change
resets" plus "a render-extent-only change derives None"; every other existing case unchanged.

## Task 4 — Resolution controller

**Model**: Sonnet 5 (review: Opus 5). **Files**: create `Source/Render/ResolutionController.h/.cpp`,
`Tests/RenderResolutionControllerTests.cpp`.

Public surface is spec section 8 verbatim (`ResolutionControllerSettings`,
`ResolutionController` with `settings()`, `setSettings()`, `scale()`, `reset()`, `declared()`,
`observe()`), pure and header-light (`<cstdint>`, `<array>`, `Temporal.h` for the scale bounds).
Implementation: a ring of eight `{frame, scale, sequence}` entries written by `declared()`; a
`sequence` counter of declared frames and `changedAtSequence` so a sample is judged only when its
entry's sequence is `> changedAtSequence + settleFrames` and its scale equals `scale()`; two
consecutive-sample counters; the rule of section 8 with `desired = scale * sqrt(target /
measured)`.

Tests (`[render][resolution]`), feeding measurements as `t = t1 * scale²` where stated: a constant
20 ms series at the default settings (target 13.6) changes nothing on the first judged sample,
steps to 0.95 on the second, then not again for `settleFrames` declared frames, and with
`t = 20 * s²` settles within one `maxStep` of `sqrt(13.6 / 20)` and never below `minScale`; a
constant 10 ms series from 0.7 steps up only on the twelfth judged sample, by at most 0.05, and
never above 1.0; a series inside `[13.6, 16]` never changes; a series alternating 15 and 17 ms
never changes; a sample whose frame was declared at an earlier scale is ignored and counts for
nothing; `reset(0.8)` answers 0.8 and clears the counters (a following single over-budget sample
does not change it); `setSettings` with `maxScale = 0.6` clamps a 0.8 scale to 0.6; a frame never
declared is ignored.

Commit: `render: add a GPU-time resolution controller with hysteresis`

## Task 5 — The upscaled frame

**Model**: Opus 5 (review: Fable, main thread). **Depends on**: Tasks 2, 3. **Files**: modify
`Source/Render/Renderer.h/.cpp`, `Source/Render/TemporalResolve.h/.cpp`,
`Shaders/TemporalReproject.slang`, `Shaders/TemporalDebugView.slang`, `Tests/GpuTemporalTests.cpp`,
`Tests/GpuRendererTests.cpp` (extent accessors only); create `Shaders/SpatialUpscale.slang`,
`Tests/Golden/frame-temporal-raw-upscaled.txt`.

1. `declarePasses`: `extents = renderExtentsForScale(m_width, m_height, temporalEnabled ?
   view.temporal.renderScale : 1.0f)` (assert the scale within `[kMinRenderScale,
   kMaxRenderScale]`); `upscaled = extents.render != extents.output`; the scene pass sets
   `renderAreaWidth/Height` only when upscaled; the sky's `jitterNdc` and the histogram's params and
   dispatch use the render extent; `displayInput` is the colour slot when `nativeTaa || upscaled`;
   `TemporalInputs::previousExtents` is the previous signature's extents, or this frame's on a reset;
   `TemporalStatus` fields of Task 3 are filled, `lastRenderExtentChangeFrame` advancing when the
   render extent differs from the previous signature's under `None`; `m_previousSceneColorUse` is
   `ShaderRead` on an upscaled `Raw` frame. Targets keep their output-extent allocation (nothing
   changes in `resize()`); `depthTarget()`/`hdrColorTarget()` comments state the active rectangle.
2. `TemporalResolve::declare`: the reprojection diagnostic and both resolve diagnostics are created
   at the output extent; `declareHistoryCommit` keeps the copy when not upscaled and otherwise
   declares `lmx.pass.temporal.commitUpscaled` — a compute pass over the output extent reading
   `sceneColor` (`shaderTextureReads`) and writing `colorSlot`, binding the clamped linear sampler
   — with `SpatialUpscale.slang`'s `computeSpatialUpscale` (params: render, output and allocated
   extents, `jitterTexelOffset`; a five-tap Catmull-Rom at `renderSamplePosition` with every tap
   clamped to `[0.5, render − 0.5]` and UVs over the allocated extent; alpha 1). `NativeTaa` when
   upscaled declares `lmx.pass.temporal.upscale` with the same reads and writes as the resolve;
   until Task 6 supplies the temporal kernel it binds the spatial pipeline as a stand-in (an
   upscaled, unaccumulated picture), so every commit renders at every scale. `recordFrame` gains `bool
   upscaled`: an upscaled `Raw` frame records `ShaderRead` for the current slot.
3. `TemporalReproject.slang`: params gain the three extents and the offset; one invocation per
   output pixel, history at `historyUv`, current colour bilinear at `renderSamplePosition`.
   `TemporalDebugView.slang`: params gain render and output extents; motion is loaded at
   `int2(pixel * render / output)` clamped to the render extent.
4. Tests: goldens — `frame-temporal-raw-upscaled.txt` at scale 0.5 shows the scene pass's `render
   area 32x32` on the 64×64 declaration scene, the `commitUpscaled` pass and no copy; `frame-
   temporal.txt`, `frame-temporal-raw.txt`, `frame-temporal-off.txt` unchanged; at scale 1 the copy
   is declared, never the spatial pass. GPU: a `Raw` frame at scale 0.5 leaves the display target
   with no clear-coloured pixel and the colour slots at the output extent; the motion of a moved
   object read back at scale 0.5 within the active rectangle matches the analytic UV delta; the
   reset case gains "a resize derives ExtentChanged, a scale change alone derives None and
   advances lastRenderExtentChangeFrame"; the in-flight overlap case runs five frames alternating
   0.5 and 1.0 with the debug view toggled, validation clean.

Commit: `render: render the scene into an active extent and upscale the raw frame`
