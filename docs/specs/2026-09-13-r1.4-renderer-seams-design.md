# Renderer Draw Stages and Compiled Records

**Status**: Implemented (2026-09-13)

This design implements the [R1.4 boundary](../roadmap/codebase-refactoring.md#r14--renderer-seams-for-m7)
under [ADR 0020](../decisions/0020-module-layering-and-units.md). It follows the initial
[milestone record](../milestones/r1.4.md) and precedes implementation.

## Draw-stage ownership

Extract concrete ShadowStage and SceneStage units on the TemporalResolve composition pattern:
creation returns an RHI Result, Renderer owns the stages, and stage declarations borrow the
frame inputs and resources through graph execution. Move existing pipeline descriptors, masked
variants, frame-data layouts and draw encoding without algebraic or binding changes. Keep sky
within the existing scene pass and preserve its ordering. Renderer remains the composition root
for targets, temporal state, exposure, bloom and display, and determines frame-wide inputs.
Keep existing public shadow-fitting helpers and capture-schema entry points compatible.
ShadowMatrices and fitShadowOrtho live with ShadowStage, included by Renderer.h. AlphaMaskParams
has one shared 4-byte layout header. Shared fallback textures and samplers remain Renderer-owned.
No new polymorphic stage framework or rendering capability is introduced.

## Compiled-record boundary

Move CompiledFrameRecord, CompiledFrameDebug and their value vocabulary into a standalone
CompiledFrameRecord.h, included by RenderGraph.h for source compatibility. Shared enumeration
and schedule definitions have one owner. Record consumers include the leaf directly, including
GraphDump, FrameRecordRing, GraphNodeModel and GraphInspectorModel.

R1.3 placed FrameDeclaration in AppModel even though it owns a RenderGraph. Move graph
construction/execution into Render, returning the accepted compiled record to the caller;
App retains that record in FrameRecordRing. Both editor and headless keep shared declaration,
pool rotation and execution, their existing begin/end/wait and motion-commit boundaries.
Tests link the Render implementation. AppModel has no direct or transitive builder dependency. Its two consumers of
`nativeOnlyTemporalView` use that unchanged pure predicate from Temporal.h rather than reaching
the graph through VendorTemporalScaler.h. The module contract explicitly forbids AppModel from
reaching RenderGraph.h, with direct/transitive negative tests.

## Compatibility and verification

No shader, RHI API, SceneView field, uniform byte layout, resource label, pass schedule or
transient assignment changes. Existing tests and checkpoint-A selection remain intact.
Fresh same-device parent/candidate builds follow the roadmap comparison protocol, including
byte artifacts, semantic captures and at most eight alternating strict parity rounds.
Candidate-only image hashes at the cap are unresolved blockers; the R1.3 exception is historical.
Exposure/bloom/display extraction, graph implementation splits and gate B remain separate work.
